## Strength and editions

Part 3 is a normative standard, but its informative annexes remain informative. OPC 11030 clause 1 expressly identifies the document as a non-mandatory whitepaper; its internal must/should/may language is retained without changing that authority boundary.

The index records the exact clauses and conditions. Do not silently substitute the latest floating edition. If the requested audit uses another edition, verify differences against that edition and report unresolved applicability.

The publisher's official publication takes precedence over its web rendering where they differ. No independent PDF-equivalence claim is made.
