# BP-211 - Phrase instance-related requirements in terms of product configurability

**Authority:** OPC 11030 is a non-mandatory whitepaper. Its own wording is retained below; a deviation is advisory unless a normative specification or an explicitly adopted policy establishes a requirement.

## Rule

Since many products are configurable, it is recommended that any requirement about instances be phrased in terms of the product being configurable to support at least one qualifying instance of the feature (rather than assuming a fixed, always-present instance).

## Applicability

Conformance Unit requirement drafting for configurable products.

## Exceptions and evidence limits

None stated.
