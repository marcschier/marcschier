# P3-180 - Choose Properties and DataVariables by meaning and extensibility

## Rule

Use Properties for metadata identified by a standard meaning/name, and DataVariables for complex values, extensible value types, or independently creatable typed values.

## Audit

Review semantics and intended operations. The Annex's decision aid is informative despite imperative wording inside its examples.
