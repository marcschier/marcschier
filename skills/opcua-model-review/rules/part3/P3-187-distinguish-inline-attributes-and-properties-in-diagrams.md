# P3-187 - Distinguish inline Attributes and Properties in diagrams

## Rule

When showing inline Attributes/Properties in Annex C extended notation, use the prescribed separated blocks, labels, and name=value entries.

## Audit

Inspect rendered figure content. A subset of Attributes may be shown; omitted optional information is not a violation.
